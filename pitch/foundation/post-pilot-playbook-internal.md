# Post-Pilot Playbook — INTERNAL (do NOT send)

> **For Marcus only.** What to do once a pilot performs. The pilot exists to manufacture the one thing
> we lack today — **KPIs** — and KPIs are the leverage for everything after. Sequence each stage to buy
> leverage for the next. Pair with `commercial-pricing-baseline.md` (reality check + negotiation room)
> and `commercial-alternatives-internal.md` (post-traction models).

## Stage 0 — Capture the numbers (the whole point)

The instant the pilot performs, package results into a **one-page case study**:

- **GGR** generated (total + per operator), average bet, hold.
- **Retention** — D1 / D7 / D30.
- **Concurrency** — peak + average concurrent players; session frequency.
- **Uplift vs. the operator's slot baseline** (the most persuasive line).

This artifact converts us from "a new studio" to "live, generating X, retaining Y." It reopens every
door — including the tier-1s (Playtech) that said "come back when proven."

> Make sure the pilot is **instrumented to produce these numbers** before it starts — analytics on GGR,
> retention, concurrency. A pilot that performs but isn't measured wastes the leverage.

## Stage 1 — Convert pilot → full rollout (same partner), and ratchet price

- Expand from 1–2 operators to the partner's wider network.
- **Renegotiate up** — this is the built-in ratchet: move rev-share from the ~12% pilot rate toward
  mid/top band (15%+); turn the infra minimum into a real floor if it was waived for the pilot; drop
  pilot concessions. Proof justifies price.
- Bake **review points** into contracts (e.g. quarterly), so price tracks proven performance.

## Stage 2 — Fund certification (the scaling gate)

- Independent cert (GLI/BMM, ~$15–50k/market, ~2–5 months) unlocks the **regulated MGA/UKGC-tier**
  operators that are off-limits today.
- With pilot revenue, the "shared milestone" becomes fundable — fund it from proven revenue and/or
  co-fund with the partner.
- **Sequence:** crypto / Curaçao operators during the pilot → certify → regulated operators.

## Stage 3 — Fan out to the other suppliers, from strength

- Re-approach the other ~19 curated suppliers with a KPI-backed opener: *"live on SOFTSWISS, generating
  X GGR, Y retention."* Converts far better than a cold pitch.
- Warm the tier-1s that wanted proof + cert first.
- The rest of the pitch kit (`suppliers/*`) is built for this — now with a case study attached.

## The strategic fork — exclusivity vs. multi-homing (decide here)

| Option | Upside | Downside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multi-home (non-exclusive)** | Maximum reach — list on SOFTSWISS *and* Hub88 *and* others; faster growth | Lower per-deal rate; no lock-in |
| **Exclusive** | Top-of-band rate **+ a real minimum guarantee** (Alternative 1); a moat for the partner | Caps reach; ties us to one distributor |

**Lean:** stay **non-exclusive early** for reach; only sell exclusivity later, at a premium, to a
partner willing to pay for the moat and clear a minimum guarantee.

## Stage 4 — Scale operations deliberately

- More operators = more load **and** more support burden. Don't promise 24×7 across 50 operators before
  we can staff it — over-committing SLAs breaks trust faster than moving slowly.
- This is where the **team / hiring** question gets real. Grow support + infra capacity *ahead of* the
  next rollout, not behind it.
- Keep infra costs tracked (GCP) so the rev-share + minimum stays profitable at scale.

## Stage 5 — Ship the roadmap (compounding)

- Leaderboards & rankings, chat badges, more game modes, tournaments — each pushes retention/GGR and
  keeps the format differentiated, which feeds better KPIs, which feeds Stage 1's ratchet. It compounds.

## Stage 6 — The endgame (optional, from strength)

- With sustained revenue, the **acquisition / IP-sale** conversation (Alternative 2) is finally worth a
  **revenue multiple**, not an acqui-hire.
- Or keep Monkify as a **rev-share cash engine** and don't sell. Either way it's a decision made from
  leverage, not desperation.

## The throughline

**Pilot → prove KPIs → ratchet price on the same partner → certify → fan out from strength → decide
exclusivity & endgame once real numbers exist.** Each stage funds and de-risks the next.
