## Slide 1 — Cover
"Thanks for the time. I'm Marcus, from Monkify. Playtech Open Platform already gives operators one
integration to hundreds of titles — so you'll recognise how this is built. In the next twenty minutes
I'll show you a finished game that's genuinely new — real-time multiplayer, not another slot — and how
it enters the Games Marketplace exactly like your other content, with a sensible, staged plan for
regulated markets."

## Slide 2 — What Monkify is
"Monkify is a real-time, multiplayer betting game. A 'monkey' types characters live, and players bet
on which sequence of letters or numbers appears first. When someone's pick shows up, they win the pot.
Everyone watches the same round together. It's not a slot and it's not a re-skin — it's a brand-new
category."

## Slide 3 — The game (screenshot)
"This is the real product, running today. The retro-arcade terminal where the round plays out, the
monkey character, and live chat. Every operator gets it skinned to their own brand."

## Slide 4 — A catalogue of modes (screenshot)
"It's not one game — it's a family of modes already built: quick races, preset-pick races, and
scheduled lotteries with growing pots. Operators can run several at once and keep the catalogue fresh."

## Slide 5 — A real win, paid on the spot (screenshot)
"Here's a round ending for real — the winning sequence appears, the terminal calls the winner, the
payout happens. The line underneath is the near-miss tracker: the player who was six characters away.
That tension is what brings people back."

## Slide 6 — Why players love it
"Four reasons it retains: it's social and competitive — you play against other people, live, not alone
against a machine. The near-miss mechanic creates real 'one more round' pull. Rounds are fast — seconds,
not minutes. And there's chat in every game."

## Slide 7 — Why it's different
"POP is brilliant at distribution and curation — but slot catalogues increasingly look alike, and
differentiation is the scarce asset. Monkify gives your operators — the bet365s, the LeoVegas of the
world — a genuinely new, non-slot talking point they can't get from another slot studio. It's a fresh
Games-Marketplace headline."

## Slide 8 — How it fits your business
"Here's the part your integration team will like. It's **one integration** to reach many operators —
the exact 'submit your game, get distributed to the network' model POP already runs. **We** operate
it: hosting, updates, 24×7 support. It **embeds** straight into the casino and carries **each
operator's branding**. For you it's a new title with very little lift."

## Slide 9 — Payments
"On money, it's deliberately simple and regulated-friendly. It settles in the **operator's own wallet**,
in USD. We hold **no player funds and no personal data** on our side — that all stays with the operator,
where the licences are. There's nothing new for your players to learn."

## Slide 10 — Fair & transparent
"On fairness I want to be straight. Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is built from
a seed the players themselves help create before the round, and any round can be independently replayed
and verified afterwards. What we do **not** yet have is independent lab certification — GLI, BMM. I'm
upfront about that. Given your operators sit in the most tightly regulated markets, our plan is a staged
one: integrate now, certify in parallel, and go live market by market as certification completes. And
the one-time setup fee is earmarked to fund that certification — so it's a funded, defined path, not a
promise."

## Slide 11 — Why work with us
"The 'why us': ready today — live, polished, proven. Support is 24×7 with clear, written response
commitments — I'll show you the SLAs. It's fully documented so operators onboard fast. And we handle
everything — you get the differentiation without the build or the run."

## Slide 12 — Commercial model
"Commercially, we've made this low-risk. We host and support the whole thing — infrastructure, updates,
and 24×7 bug-fixing are all on us, so there's nothing for you or your operators to run. We'd start with
a **pilot** on one or two operators to prove it before scaling across POP. We earn from a **share of the
revenue** the game makes. And there's a small **monthly minimum** that just covers the dedicated
infrastructure and support we run — **credited against your revenue share**, so in any decent month you
only pay the share. Setup is waived. ⟨aside, not on slide: 12% of GGR and a ~$2,000/month minimum are the
starting numbers — all open to discussion, and the minimum can be waived for the pilot.⟩ It's built to be
an easy yes."

## Slide 13 — What's next
"Not a one-off title. On the roadmap: leaderboards and player rankings, player badges and status in
chat, more game modes, and tournaments and seasonal events. A living product — a reason to start the
relationship now."

### Q&A quick-reference (say these if asked)
- **"Are you certified?"** — "Not yet, and I won't pretend otherwise. Provably fair by design and
  replayable today; independent lab certification is the next milestone, funded by the setup fee, and we
  stage go-live market by market as it completes."
- **"Do you support crypto?"** — "The game can, but for Playtech we're proposing regulated, fiat-wallet
  settlement only — no crypto surface." (Don't lead with crypto here.)
- **"What's the lift for us?"** — "One integration through POP; we run and support it. Your team
  integrates against a sandbox first."
- **"How big is your team?"** — deflect to capability: "We provide 24×7 support with SLAs; that's the
  commitment that matters." (Never state headcount.)
