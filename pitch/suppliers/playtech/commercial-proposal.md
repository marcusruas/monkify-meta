# Commercial Proposal — Monkify × Playtech

> **Confidential — shared under NDA.** Prepared July 2026. All amounts in USD. Figures below are our
> proposed terms and remain **open to discussion** to fit Playtech's scale and market mix.

## Summary

We propose to make Monkify available to Playtech and its operator network via the **Playtech Open
Platform / Games Marketplace**, as a **licensed, fully-operated service**: we host it, support it 24×7,
integrate your operators, and take a share of the revenue the game generates. One integration adds a
differentiated, non-slot title to your catalogue with minimal lift.

## The offer

| Component | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| **One-time setup** | **$25,000**, earmarked to **fund independent game certification** (see "Certification", below). |
| **Monthly fee (fixed)** | **$6,000 / month**, covering 24×7 technical support **and all hosting/infrastructure** — a single fixed amount, never a variable infrastructure bill. |
| **Revenue share** | **15% of GGR** (gross gaming revenue) generated by Monkify across your operators. |
| **Included** | Hosting, 24×7 support, operator onboarding, content updates, full integration documentation, and the embeddable game. |

> These are our proposed terms and are **open to negotiation** against Playtech's reach and volume.

## Settlement — regulated and fiat

Monkify settles in the **operator's own wallet, in USD** — off-chain. We hold **no player funds and no
player personal data**; that stays with the operator, where the licences are. There is nothing new for
your players to learn, and no crypto surface in the Playtech integration.

## Service-level commitments (SLAs)

Included in the monthly fee:

| Area | Commitment |
| --- | --- |
| **Critical / game-blocking bug** | Acknowledged within **1 hour**; workaround or fix within **24 hours** (24×7). |
| **Standard bug** | Acknowledged within **1 business day**; resolved in the next release or within **5 business days**. |
| **New operator integration** | Live within **10 business days** of receiving the operator's credentials and configuration. |
| **New features** | Requests assessed within **5 business days**; scheduled feature releases at least **quarterly**. |

## Certification — transparent, funded by setup, staged for POP's markets

We want to be straightforward about where the game stands:

- Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is generated from a seed that players
  themselves contribute to before the round begins, and **any round can be independently replayed and
  verified** afterwards.
- Monkify is **not yet independently lab-certified** (e.g. GLI / BMM). We're upfront about that.
- The **one-time setup fee is earmarked to fund that certification** — turning a cost that any new
  content carries into a clear, funded path to certified go-live.
- Because POP's operators sit in the most tightly regulated markets, we propose a **staged plan:
  integrate now, certify in parallel, and go live market by market as certification completes.**

## What Playtech gets

- A finished, polished, non-slot title — **one integration to the POP operator network**.
- Regulated-friendly **fiat wallet settlement** in USD; no player funds or PII on our side.
- **24×7 support with the SLAs above**; we operate and maintain everything.
- Full documentation for your operators, and a fresh Games-Marketplace headline.
- A forward roadmap: leaderboards & rankings, chat badges, more game modes, tournaments.
