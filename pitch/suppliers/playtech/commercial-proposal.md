# Commercial Proposal — Monkify × Playtech

> **Confidential — shared under NDA.** Prepared July 2026. All amounts in USD. Terms below are our
> proposed starting point and are **open to discussion** to fit Playtech's scale and market mix.

## A low-risk way to add a brand-new game to POP

We make this easy to say yes to: **we host, run, and fully support Monkify end to end** — you simply
distribute it through the Playtech Open Platform. We start with a **pilot**, and we earn primarily from a
**share of the revenue** the game generates. No platform to build, nothing for your operators to operate.

## What we handle — so you and your operators don't

- **All infrastructure & hosting.** Monkify runs entirely on infrastructure we provision, scale, and
  monitor. Nothing to host on your side.
- **All technical support & bug-fixing, 24×7.** We own maintenance, incident response, and fixes (see
  SLAs below).
- **Operator onboarding, content updates, and full documentation** — we run the integration and keep it
  healthy.

## The offer

| Component | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| **Start with a pilot** | A time-boxed pilot on **1–2 operators** — prove performance with minimal commitment before scaling across POP. |
| **Revenue share** | **12% of the GGR** (gross gaming revenue) Monkify generates. Open to discussion and can scale with volume. |
| **Monthly minimum** | **$2,000 / month**, which covers the **dedicated infrastructure and 24×7 support we run for you**. It is **credited against your revenue share** — so in any month your GGR share exceeds it, you pay only the share; the minimum simply ensures the platform we operate is covered in a low-volume month. |
| **Setup / integration** | **Waived** — we absorb integration to keep the barrier low. |

> These are proposed starting terms and are **open to negotiation** against Playtech's reach and volume.

## Service-level commitments (SLAs) — we own support

Because we handle all support, we commit to it in writing:

| Area | Commitment |
| --- | --- |
| **Critical / game-blocking bug** | Acknowledged within **1 hour**; workaround or fix within **24 hours** (24×7). |
| **Standard bug** | Acknowledged within **1 business day**; resolved in the next release or within **5 business days**. |
| **New operator integration** | Live within **10 business days** of receiving the operator's credentials and configuration. |
| **New features** | Requests assessed within **5 business days**; scheduled feature releases at least **quarterly**. |

## Settlement — regulated and fiat

Monkify settles in the **operator's own wallet, in USD** — off-chain. We hold **no player funds and no
player personal data**; that stays with the operator, where the licences are. There is nothing new for
your players to learn, and no crypto surface in the Playtech integration.

## Certification — transparent, staged for POP's markets

Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is generated from a seed players contribute to
before the round, and any round can be **independently replayed and verified**. We're upfront that it is
**not yet independently lab-certified** (e.g. GLI / BMM). Because POP's operators sit in tightly regulated
markets, we propose a **staged plan: pilot and integrate now, certify in parallel as a shared milestone,
and go live market by market as certification completes.**

## What Playtech gets

- A finished, differentiated, non-slot title — **one integration to the POP operator network**, with
  **zero operational load** on your side (we host and support everything).
- Regulated-friendly **fiat wallet settlement** in USD; no player funds or PII on our side.
- **24×7 support with the SLAs above.**
- A fresh Games-Marketplace headline, and a forward roadmap: leaderboards & rankings, chat badges, more
  game modes, tournaments.

## The path from here

We see this as a partnership that grows in stages:

1. **Pilot** — prove performance on 1–2 operators.
2. **Rollout** — expand across the POP operator network.
3. **Certified expansion** — complete certification as a shared milestone and go live market by market
   across your regulated operators.
4. **A growing library** — new game modes and features (leaderboards, tournaments, badges) keep the
   content fresh.
