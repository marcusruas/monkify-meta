## Cover
"Thanks for the time. I'm Marcus, from Monkify. BC.Game built its name on originals — Crash, Dice,
Plinko — and last year you licensed Onlyplay's TapGame because novel mechanics out-earn slots. Monkify
is the next step on that exact thesis: a genuinely new, real-time *multiplayer* game that nobody in your
BC Originals lineup — or your 60-studio roster — has. In the next twenty minutes I'll show it to you and
how it drops into your lobby."

## What Monkify is
"A real-time, multiplayer betting game. A 'monkey' types characters live, players bet on which sequence
appears first, and the moment a pick shows up they win the pot. Everyone watches the same round together.
It's not a slot and it's not a solo instant game — it's a brand-new category."

## The game (screenshot)
"This is the real product, running today — the retro-arcade terminal where the round plays out, plus live
chat. It gets skinned to BC.Game's brand and sits right alongside your originals."

## A catalogue of modes (screenshot)
"It's a family of modes already built — quick races, preset-pick races, and scheduled lotteries with
growing pots. You can run several at once and keep it fresh."

## A real win, paid on the spot (screenshot)
"A real round ending — the winning sequence lands, the winner is called, the payout happens. The line
underneath is the near-miss tracker, the player who was closest. That tension is the retention hook —
the same 'one more round' pull your Crash players know."

## Why players love it
"Social and competitive — players compete against each other, live, not alone against a machine. The
near-miss mechanic creates real 'one more round' pull. Rounds are fast — seconds. And there's live chat
in every game."

## Why it's different
"You already proved the thesis with TapGame: novel, high-engagement mechanics beat standard slots.
Monkify takes it further — it's real-time *multiplayer*, which is a genre nobody has, including you. And
it's provably fair in the same spirit as your hash-chain originals, so it fits your brand's DNA."

## A new original, live in your lobby
"For your team it's almost no lift. One integration puts it live for your 9M+ players. We host it, we
update it, we support it 24×7. It carries your branding and sits with your BC Originals. You get a
differentiated original without an internal build."

## Payments — crypto-native, plus fiat
"It's crypto-native by design. On-chain settlement is already built — Solana today, dropping straight
into your wallet stack, and we're open to adding more tokens as you need them. There's also a full
off-chain wallet path if you'd rather settle in balances. Either way, one game — we fit your model."

## Fair & transparent
"On fairness: Monkify is provably fair by design — every result is built from a seed players contribute
to before the round, and any round can be independently replayed and verified, exactly like your
originals' hash-chain model. Independent lab certification is on our roadmap for when we expand into
regulated markets — but it's nothing you need for your players. I'll always be upfront about where we
are."

## Why work with us
"Ready today — live, polished, proven. 24×7 support with written SLAs. Fully documented so the
integration is fast and low-lift. And we handle everything — you get the differentiation without the
build or the run."

## Commercial model
"Low-risk. We host and support the whole thing — infrastructure, updates, 24×7 bug-fixing all on us.
We'd start with a pilot so you can see it perform. We earn from a share of the revenue the game makes.
And there's a small monthly minimum that just covers the infrastructure and support we run — credited
against your revenue share, so in any decent month you only pay the share. Setup is waived. ⟨aside, not
on slide: 12% of GGR and a ~$2,000/month minimum are the starting numbers — all open to discussion, and
the minimum can be waived for the pilot.⟩ Built to be an easy yes."

## What's next
"Not a one-off: leaderboards and player rankings, player badges in chat, more game modes, tournaments
and seasonal events. A living original — a reason to lock it in now."
