# Commercial Proposal — Monkify × BC.Game

> **Confidential — shared under NDA.** Prepared July 2026. All amounts in USD (settlement can be in your
> crypto units). Terms below are our proposed starting point and are **open to discussion** to fit
> BC.Game.

## A low-risk way to add a brand-new original

We make this easy to say yes to: **we host, run, and fully support Monkify end to end** — it goes live
in your lobby with almost no lift for your team. We start with a **pilot**, and we earn primarily from a
**share of the revenue** the game generates. No platform to build, nothing for your team to operate.

## What we handle — so your team doesn't

- **All infrastructure & hosting.** Monkify runs entirely on infrastructure we provision, scale, and
  monitor.
- **All technical support & bug-fixing, 24×7.** We own maintenance, incident response, and fixes (SLAs
  below).
- **The integration, content updates, and full documentation** — we run it and keep it healthy.

## The offer

| Component | Terms |
| --- | --- |
| **Start with a pilot** | A time-boxed pilot live in your lobby — prove performance with your players before committing further. |
| **Revenue share** | **12% of the GGR** (gross gaming revenue) Monkify generates for BC.Game. Open to discussion and can scale with volume. |
| **Monthly minimum** | **$2,000 / month**, which covers the **dedicated infrastructure and 24×7 support we run**. It is **credited against your revenue share** — so in any month your GGR share exceeds it, you pay only the share; the minimum simply ensures the platform we operate is covered in a low-volume month. |
| **Setup / integration** | **Waived** — we absorb integration to keep the barrier low. |

> These are proposed starting terms and are **open to negotiation** — the minimum can be waived for the
> pilot itself.

## Service-level commitments (SLAs) — we own support

| Area | Commitment |
| --- | --- |

| **Standard bug** | Acknowledged within **1 business day**; resolved in the next release or within **5 business days**. |
| **New game mode / config** | Live within **10 business days** of agreeing scope. |
| **New features** | Requests assessed within **5 business days**; scheduled feature releases at least **quarterly**. |

## Settlement — crypto-native, with a fiat path

- **On-chain** — settles on-chain and drops into your wallet stack. **Solana today, and we're open to
  adding more chains and tokens as you need them.**
- **Off-chain wallet path** — available if you'd rather settle against player balances (we call your
  wallet API). Your choice, per game.

## Certification — transparent

Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is generated from a seed players contribute to
before the round, and any round can be **independently replayed and verified**, the same self-verifiable
spirit as your hash-chain BC Originals. It is **not yet independently lab-certified**; that's on our
roadmap for regulated-market expansion and is **nothing your players need**.

## What BC.Game gets

- A finished, differentiated **original** — one integration, live for your 9M+ players, with **zero
  operational load** on your side (we host and support everything).
- **Crypto-native settlement** (Solana today, more as needed) plus an off-chain path.
- **24×7 support with the SLAs above.**
- A forward roadmap: leaderboards & rankings, chat badges, more game modes, tournaments.

## The path from here

We see this as a partnership that grows in stages:

1. **Pilot** — prove performance with your players.
2. **Expand** — add game modes and grow the placement in your lobby.
3. **Roadmap** — new formats and features (leaderboards, tournaments, badges) keep it fresh.
4. **Regulated markets, later** — we complete certification as we grow, opening additional markets — at
   your option, and nothing you need now.
