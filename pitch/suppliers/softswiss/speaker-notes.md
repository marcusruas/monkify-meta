## Slide 1 — Cover
"Thanks for making the time. I'm Marcus, from Monkify. SOFTSWISS is the aggregator that put crypto
gaming on the map and now runs one of the biggest content libraries in the industry — so you're
exactly who I built this for. In the next twenty minutes I'll show you a finished game that's
genuinely new, and how it plugs into your single-API model for both your fiat and your crypto
operators."

## Slide 2 — What Monkify is
"Monkify is a real-time, multiplayer betting game. A 'monkey' types characters live, and players bet
on which sequence of letters or numbers will appear first. The moment someone's pick shows up, they
win the pot. Everyone's watching the same round at the same time — it's shared and social. It is not a
slot, not a re-skin — it's a brand-new category."

## Slide 3 — The game (screenshot)
"This is the real product, running today — not a mock-up. You've got the retro-arcade terminal on the
left where the round plays out, the monkey character, and live chat on the right. Every operator gets
this skinned to their own brand."

## Slide 4 — A catalogue of modes (screenshot)
"And it's not one game — it's a family of modes already built: quick races, preset-pick races, and
scheduled lotteries with growing pots. Same engine, different rhythms, so operators can run several at
once and keep it fresh."

## Slide 5 — A real win, paid on the spot (screenshot)
"Here's the moment that matters — a real round ending. The winning sequence appears, the terminal
calls the winner, and the payout happens immediately. That 'so close' line underneath is the near-miss
tracker — the player who was six characters away. That tension is what brings people back."

## Slide 6 — Why players love it
"Four reasons this retains: it's social and competitive — you're playing *against other people*, live,
not alone against a machine. The near-miss mechanic creates genuine 'one more round' pull. Rounds are
fast — seconds, not minutes. And there's chat in every game, so it's a place to hang out, not just
bet."

## Slide 7 — Why it's different
"You already know this works — SOFTSWISS describes crash as 'multiplayer by default: players see each
other's cash-outs, chat, watch together.' Monkify is the next step in exactly that appetite: real-time,
social, competitive — but a completely new mechanic, not another crash clone. For a 40,000-title
catalogue that's mostly slots, it's a genuine category-first — and, frankly, great press-release
material, which I know you publish constantly."

## Slide 8 — How it fits your business
"Here's the part your team will like. It's **one integration** to reach your whole network — the same
'one API, many operators' model you already run. **We** operate it: hosting, updates, and 24×7 support
are on us. It **embeds** straight into the casino, and it carries **each operator's branding**. For
you, it's a new title in the library with almost no lift."

## Slide 9 — Payments (fiat + crypto)
"It works both ways. For your regulated, fiat operators, it settles in the operator's own wallet in
USD — nothing exotic. For your crypto operators, it settles on-chain — and this is where your heritage
comes in: Monkify is crypto-native. **Today we settle in Solana tokens, and we're happy to add more
chains and tokens as your operators need them.** Same game either way — you pick per game."

## Slide 10 — Fair & transparent
"On fairness I want to be straight with you. Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is
built from a seed that the players themselves help create before the round starts, and any round can be
independently replayed and verified afterwards. What we do **not** yet have is independent lab
certification — GLI, BMM, that level. I'm upfront about that. Our plan is simple: the one-time setup
fee is earmarked to fund that certification. So you're not carrying a cost you'd carry for any new
content anyway — you're funding a clear path to certified go-live, and getting a differentiated game
in return. And your crypto and Curaçao operators can go live first while that completes."

## Slide 11 — Why work with us
"To sum up the 'why us': the game is ready today — live, polished, proven. Support is 24×7 with
clear, written response commitments — I'll show you the SLAs. It's fully documented so your operators
onboard fast. And we handle everything — you get the differentiation without the build or the run."

## Slide 12 — Commercial model
"Commercially, we've made this low-risk. We host and support the whole thing — infrastructure, updates,
and 24×7 bug-fixing are all on us, so there's nothing for you or your operators to run. We'd start with
a **pilot** on one or two operators, so you can see it perform before scaling to the network. We earn
from a **share of the revenue** the game makes. And there's a small **monthly minimum** that just covers
the dedicated infrastructure and support we run for you — and it's **credited against your revenue
share**, so in any decent month you only pay the share. Setup is waived. ⟨aside, not on slide: 12% of GGR
and a ~$2,000/month minimum are the starting numbers — all open to discussion, and the minimum can be
waived for the pilot.⟩ It's built to be an easy yes."

## Slide 13 — What's next
"This isn't a one-off title. On the roadmap: leaderboards and player rankings, player badges and status
in chat, more game modes, and tournaments and seasonal events. It's a living product — a reason to lock
a relationship in now."
