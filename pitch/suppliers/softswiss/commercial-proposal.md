# Commercial Proposal — Monkify × SOFTSWISS

> **Confidential — shared under NDA.** Prepared July 2026. All amounts in USD. Figures below are our
> proposed terms and remain **open to discussion** to fit SOFTSWISS's scale and market mix.

## Summary

We propose to make Monkify available across the **SOFTSWISS Game Aggregator** as a **licensed,
fully-operated service**: we host it, support it 24×7, integrate your operators, and take a share of
the revenue the game generates. One integration puts a differentiated, crypto-native game in front of
your whole operator network.

| Component | Terms |

> These are our proposed terms and are **open to negotiation** against SOFTSWISS's reach and volume.

## Settlement — fiat and crypto

Monkify serves both sides of your network from one integration:

- **Regulated / fiat operators** — settles off-chain in the operator's own wallet, in **USD**.
- **Crypto operators** — settles **on-chain**. **Today we settle in Solana tokens, and we're open to
  adding more chains and tokens as your operators need them.**

## Certification — transparent, and funded by setup

We want to be straightforward about where the game stands:

- Monkify is **provably fair by design** — every result is generated from a seed that players
  themselves contribute to before the round begins, and **any round can be independently replayed and
  verified** afterwards.
- Monkify is **not yet independently lab-certified** (e.g. GLI / BMM). We're upfront about that.
- The **one-time setup fee is earmarked to fund that certification** — turning a cost that any new
  content carries into a clear, funded path to certified go-live.
- Your **crypto and Curaçao operators can go live first** while certification completes for MGA-tier
  regulated listings.
