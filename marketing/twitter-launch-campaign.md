## 4. Organic growth playbook ($0)

> **Set honest expectations first.** Real organic growth is slow: **0→1K followers typically takes 1–2 months**,
> and X's 2026 feed now structurally favors **Premium** accounts (verified accounts see a large reach multiplier
> over brand-new free ones). None of the tactics below cost money. The **one** honest, optional exception — clearly
> outside this $0 plan — is that a single **X Premium** subscription on @SpaceStroker is the highest-leverage dollar
> you could ever spend on reach; decide that separately. Everything else here is free.

### 4.1 Account setup & optimisation
Handle `@SpaceStroker`, display name "Space Stroker 🐵", the §1 bio with **`[PLAY_URL]` as the link-in-bio**, Edson
profile pic, a space/arcade banner, and the **launch thread pinned** on T-0. A cold visitor must "get it" in 2
seconds: *bet USDC on what a monkey types, provably fair, on Solana.*

### 4.2 Cold-start seeding — the first hour of every post
You're starting near zero, so never post into an empty room:
- All **3 crew** (Bidi, Ryugaki, Bolso) + close friends **like + reply + repost within ~15 min** of each post.
- Seed the **first reply** yourself (it's also where `[PLAY_URL]` goes on link tweets).
- Ask your circle to **quote-tweet** the launch thread in their own words — quotes outperform bare reposts.

### 4.3 Where the audience is (verify each handle before engaging)
Show up where Solana degens and gamers already are. **Verify the exact handle/community URL before you engage —
crypto is full of look-alike/impersonator accounts** (e.g. capital-I `@SoIanaDegen` vs a real one).

**The named targets live in [`marketing/promotion-channels.md`](./promotion-channels.md)** — a live-verified,
tiered directory built for exactly this section. Don't go hunting; don't rebuild a list here.

- Work **§2 (Tier 1)** top to bottom — it's ordered, and it's cross-platform.
- **X accounts to reply under are §5.4**; **X Communities to post *into* are §5.5.** With a new account the
  Communities matter more — they're the one place on X where follower count isn't the bottleneck.
- **Check the `Self-promo` column before you post anything.** Some rooms ban project links outright.
- Entries carry an `as of` date and decay — **re-verify per §6** before each wave.

That directory settles the names this section used to recommend from memory. **`@solanagaming`** holds up and is
now the top X reply target; **"Solana Meme Coin"** is real and the largest on-axis X Community (37.6k). But

### 4.4 Reply / quote-tweet strategy (your #1 lever)
On X in 2026, **replies are weighted far more heavily than likes** for reach. So the plan is reply-first:
- Add **genuine, funny, Edson-voiced** replies to larger Solana gaming/casino/degen posts — value or a joke, never
  "check out my project" spam.
- **Quote-tweet** relevant threads (a fairness debate, a "is X rigged?" post) with the Space Stroker angle.
- 20–40 quality replies/day beats 1 more original tweet. This is where a 0-follower account actually gets seen.

### 4.5 Hashtags
**1–2 max** (`#Solana`, `#SolanaGaming`). Three or more reads as spam to the algorithm and *reduces* reach. Often
best in a reply or the thread tail, not the hook.

### 4.6 Best times to post
Crypto CT is busiest **Tue–Thu, ~9am–3pm ET**; add an **evening slot** for EU/Asia. Post the **launch thread in a
prime weekday-midday window**. Then watch your own analytics for a week and lean into what actually lands for
*your* audience.

### 4.7 Free KOL / micro-influencer outreach (no paid promo)
Target **small Solana-gaming creators** (a few thousand followers — they reply and amplify; mega-KOLs ignore
cold DMs). A short, honest DM:
> "Hey — we're 3 devs launching Space Stroker, a provably-fair, token-free USDC betting game on Solana (bet on
> what a monkey types 🐵). No budget, just built something we love — want a first look / a couple of free
> rounds to try? No ask, no strings."
Offer early access and a genuine look; **never** promise payment you can't make, and don't fake a partnership.

### 4.8 Ongoing content cadence
A repeatable weekly loop: **memes** (Edson + degen culture) · **lore drops** · **mode clips** · **polls** ("what
does Edson type next?") · **"gm" / community banter** · **real winning-round screenshots** (real only — see §6).
Aim 2–5 posts/day, mostly lightweight, with the reply grind underneath.

### 4.9 Precedent scan — what worked for comparable Solana apps
- **Moonbet (Solana-built casino):** made **transparency the marketing** — on-chain, provably-fair outcomes,
  odds/RTP shown up front, and **no bonus gimmicks**. → *Lead with provably-fair + instant payouts + "no token,
  no presale". It's our strongest, most honest hook — and it directly answers the degen's "is it rigged?" fear.*
- **Referral-driven casinos (e.g. Slotana-style):** grew via **affiliate/referral loops**. → *We deliberately have
  no giveaway/referral in scope now; keep it as a **future** growth lever, not a launch tactic.*
- **Memecoin/community launches (BONK / WIF / pump.fun culture):** won on **community + lore + memes first, product
  second**. → *We already have Edson's lore and an arcade meme identity — lean on it hard; the story is the
  shareable asset.*
- **General lesson across Solana launches:** authenticity + a genuine community beat paid hype. A tiny, loud,
  in-on-the-joke community outperforms a big cold ad spend at this stage.

### 4.10 Platform-safety notes
- X restricts **paid** gambling promotion; we run **$0/organic**, so no ads anyway. Keep **links in bio/replies**,
  not tweet bodies (reach + compliance).
- Keep a light **responsible-gaming** sensibility (real money, 18+; "bet what you can afford to lose"); never
  target minors.
- **Token-free integrity:** never post a ticker/contract address; if a token comes up, use the **"No token. Yet." +
  anti-scam** line ("anyone selling a '$SPACE STROKER' today is not us").

## 6. Guardrails & pre-post checklist

**Never post:**
- ❌ Financial promises — "guaranteed", "risk-free", ROI/%/multiplier ("2x", "100x"), "get rich", "you'll win",
  "can't lose", "passive income". (Betting language is fine — *outcome* promises are not.)
- ❌ Fabricated metrics or fake social proof — invented player counts, pot sizes, winners, or testimonials. Post a
  number **only** after it truly happens and is verifiable.
- ❌ A token **ticker or contract address**, or any hint that a token/airdrop is live or buyable. Use "No token.
  Yet." + the anti-scam line instead.
- ❌ A raw app link in a **main** tweet body — link lives in the **bio** and **first reply**.
- ❌ Claims about features that aren't live on launch day.

**Always:** brand "Space Stroker", handle @SpaceStroker, real USDC on Solana, provably-fair, responsible/18+ tone.

**Pre-post self-audit** (run before posting a batch, from the `monkify-meta` worktree):

```bash
grep -niE "guarantee|risk[- ]free|\broi\b|get rich|[0-9]+ ?x\b|to the moon|can'?t lose|\$SPACE|airdrop is live|passive income" marketing/twitter-launch-campaign.md
```
Expect **no** hits outside this guardrail section. Also eyeball: every tweet ≤280 chars, the bio ≤160, and every
`[PLAY_URL]` either replaced or intentionally left as "link in bio".
