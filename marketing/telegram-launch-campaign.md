## 4. Organic growth playbook ($0)

> **Set honest expectations first.** A crypto Telegram group grows slowly and by trust, not hype — modern Telegram
> growth is opt-in exposure through relevant communities + consistency, **not** mass DMs or spam (which get you
> banned and reported). None of the tactics below cost money. Your single biggest lever is that Telegram is where a
> Solana community actually *lives* — so make it a place worth staying.

### 4.1 Group setup & optimisation (see §6)
Public group, claimed `[GROUP_URL]`, the §1 description with `[PLAY_URL]`, Edson group photo, the **pinned welcome**
(with PLAY NOW button) and the **pinned safety notice**, a management bot + **join captcha**. A cold visitor must
"get it" in 2 seconds from the pins: *bet SOL on what a monkey types, provably fair, on Solana, no token.*

### 4.2 Cold-start seeding — never an empty room
You're starting near zero. An empty, silent group kills trust instantly, so before you invite anyone wider:
- All **3 crew** (Bidi, Ryugaki, Bolso) + close friends **join, say gm, and hold a real short conversation** — seed
  5–10 genuine messages and one poll so the first outsider sees a *living* room.
- Set the bot's **welcome message** (§6) so every new joiner is greeted and pointed at the pins automatically.
- First-hour of each announcement: crew react + reply so the message has life on it.

### 4.3 The X → Telegram funnel (your #1 growth source — cross-references [#76](https://trello.com/c/gApr5z7v))
Twitter/X is top-of-funnel **reach**; this group is where that reach **lands and stays**. The two campaigns are one
launch. Concrete tactics:
- Put `[GROUP_URL]` in the **X bio** and in the **pinned X launch thread**.
- Add a **first reply** to the X launch thread (tweet **T0-6** in #76): *"Join the gang on Telegram 👉 [GROUP_URL]"*.
- **Mirror** each X announcement into the group (and vice-versa) so the two feel like one community.
- Wire the **LP `TELEGRAM` social link** to `[GROUP_URL]` — it's currently a `#` placeholder / `TODO(ship)` in
  `monkify-lp/src/pages/index.astro`. *(That LP edit is a separate task — not part of this campaign — but flag it so
  it happens before launch.)*
- This funnel is **additive**: if the X campaign slips, this Telegram plan still runs on its own.

### 4.4 Where the audience is (verify every group before you engage)
Solana communities form fast around new projects and Telegram is often their first stop. Show up where they already
are — **but verify each group/handle first; impersonation and look-alike groups are everywhere.**

**The named groups live in [`marketing/promotion-channels.md`](./promotion-channels.md)** — a live-verified, tiered
directory built for exactly this section. Don't go directory-hunting; don't rebuild a list here.

- **Telegram entries are §5.2**, with member counts, online counts and a self-promo rule each.
- Work **§2 (Tier 1)** first — it's cross-platform, so the Telegram rooms are ranked against everything else.
- Entries carry an `as of` date and decay — **re-verify per §6** before each wave.

Read §5.2's verdict before you spend an evening on this: the research pass found only **6** Telegram rooms worth
listing (against a target of 8), because large public "Solana communities" turned out to be substantially a
**coordinated pump funnel** — four separate channels, ~710k subscribers between them, all posting the same token at

### 4.8 Free KOL / group-admin outreach (no paid promo)
Target **small** Solana-gaming creators and **friendly group admins** (a few thousand members — they actually reply

Offer a genuine look; **never** promise payment you can't make, and don't fake a partnership.

### 4.9 Precedent scan — how comparable Solana apps run Telegram
- **Provably-fair Solana casinos (e.g. Solpump):** make **transparency the pitch** — connect wallet, on-chain
  provably-fair outcomes, instant settling, verify-it-yourself. → *Lead with provably-fair + instant payouts + "no
  token, no presale". It's our strongest, most honest hook and it directly answers the degen's "is it rigged?" fear.*
- **In-Telegram casino bots (e.g. BC.Game's TG bot):** many Solana casinos run the *whole game inside a Telegram
  bot*. → *We deliberately don't — Space Stroker is a web app; our group is a **community + funnel to `[PLAY_URL]`**,
  not a gambling bot. Own that difference; a real community outlasts a bot.*
- **Community-oriented casino channels (e.g. Betplay-style):** regular promo/tournament posts + an active chat. →
  *Keep a light, steady cadence (lore, polls, mode spotlights); an active, in-on-the-joke room beats a firehose.*
- **Meme-token → casino ecosystems:** win on **community + lore + memes first, product second**. → *We already have
  Edson's lore and an arcade identity — lean on it hard; the story is the shareable asset.*
- **General lesson:** on Solana, an authentic, loud, small community + a captcha-guarded safe room beats cold spend
  and beats a group that gets scam-raided on day one.

### 4.10 Platform-safety notes
- Telegram **permits links**, so put `[PLAY_URL]` in messages, the button, and the pin — the real risk is **scams

## 6. Group setup, moderation & anti-scam

**This is the section that keeps your community from becoming a scam farm.** Crypto Telegram groups are prime targets:
impersonator "admins" who DM your members, scammers posting fake contract addresses, wallet-drainer "airdrop" links,
and bot raids on the join. A safe, well-moderated room is part of launching — do this **before** you invite anyone.

### 6.1 Group creation & setup checklist
- [ ] Create a **public group** (so it has a shareable `t.me/…` link) and **claim its username** early — `[GROUP_URL]`
      — before a squatter takes a look-alike.
- [ ] Set **display name** `Space Stroker 🐵`, the **group photo** (`edson.png`, static square), and the **≤255-char
      description** from §1.
- [ ] **Pin two messages:** the §1 welcome, then the §6.4 safety notice.
- [ ] Enable **reactions**; consider **slow mode** (e.g. 10–30s) to blunt spam/raids.
- [ ] Restrict **new members** from posting **links / media / forwards** for their first hours (a bot can auto-lift
      after N minutes or a message threshold) — the single biggest reducer of drive-by scam posts.
- [ ] Add your admins, and **keep the real admin list short + pinned** (see §6.4).
