# Monkify — Anti-clone positioning playbook

> **Version:** 1 · **Date:** 2026-07-01
> **Audience:** Internal sales / business-development
> **Confidentiality:** Internal.
> **Status:** Draft — not legal advice.

## Purpose

An internal playbook that makes **licensing Monkify easier than cloning it**. The game mechanic
("bet on the first word a monkey types") is simple to describe, so a prospect may think "we'll just
build it." This document is the set of arguments and moves that make buying the faster, cheaper,
lower-risk choice — plus a **pre-sales-call checklist**. It is commercial/GTM positioning, not a
legal instrument (we do **not** rely on patents; see Out of scope).

## The five levers

### 1. First reference operator (social proof)
One live deployment is worth more than any pitch. **Target** a first launch — a crypto-native
operator is the fastest path (lighter document demands, Model B integration). Once live, it becomes
"operator X runs Monkify" in every later conversation.

> Framing rule: until a deployment is actually live, speak of it as a **target / pilot**, never as an
> existing client. Never name a casino as a Monkify customer before it is one.

### 2. Exclusivity as a paid add-on
Offer **exclusivity** (by territory, vertical, or operator group) as a **paid** contract add-on — not
free, never implicit. It turns "we could clone it" into "if we don't license it, a competitor gets
the exclusive." Time-box it and tie renewal to performance.

### 3. Speed to launch
Licensing Monkify is **weeks to integrate**; building an equivalent internally is **months to build
+ QA + (for regulated buyers) certify**, plus ongoing maintenance. Lead with the delta: time-to-first-
revenue, not feature lists.

### 4. Brand
Players and operators ask for **Monkify** and **Edson** by name — not "the word typer game." The
mechanic is cloneable; the brand, mascot, and recognition are not. Lean on the identity, the live
spectacle, and the community hook.

### 5. Cost framing (rebuild vs SaaS)
Frame the real comparison honestly:

| | Build internally | License Monkify (SaaS) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upfront | Salaried dev + design + QA over months | Setup fee (editable) |
| Ongoing | Your team maintains, fixes, runs it | Monthly platform fee (hosting + fixes + support) |
| Regulated readiness | You build + fund certification | On the roadmap; certification **DEFERRED** until funded |
| Time to first revenue | Months | Weeks |

Pricing is **setup + monthly** (no revenue share) — see the pitch doc for the buyer's track
([Model A](model-a/01-pitch.md) / [Model B](model-b/01-pitch.md)).

## Pre-sales-call checklist

Run this before every prospect call:

- [ ] **Identify the buyer's track** — regulated (Model A) or crypto-native (Model B)? It changes the
      whole conversation (wallet, KYC, RG, document demands).
- [ ] **Send the right doc first** — the pitch for the buyer's track ([Model A](model-a/01-pitch.md) /
      [Model B](model-b/01-pitch.md)) is cold-sendable. The matching integration overview
      ([Model A](model-a/02-integration-overview.md) / [Model B](model-b/02-integration-overview.md))
      goes **only after a signed NDA**.
- [ ] **NDA gate confirmed** — do not share the integration overview, source, credentials, or internal
      architecture before a mutual NDA + non-use is signed.
- [ ] **Pricing straight** — setup + monthly; **no revenue share / NGR**; new features billed
      separately; exclusivity is a paid add-on; 12-month minimum. Have the editable figures ready.
- [ ] **Reference posture** — only speak of live deployments that are actually live; others are
      "targets/pilots."
- [ ] **Deferred items honest** — lab RNG certification (GLI/BMM), lawyer review, trademark are
      **DEFERRED**; present them as roadmap, never as done.
- [ ] **Next step defined** — the goal of a first call is to book the technical deep-dive (under NDA),
      not to close.
- [ ] **Anti-clone lever chosen** — pick the one or two levers above that fit this buyer (speed for a
      time-pressured operator; exclusivity for a competitive market; cost for a budget-conscious one).

## Out of scope

- **Patents** on the game mechanic — not pursued.
- **Legal enforcement** against concept clones — not a strategy here; positioning does the work.
- Any real client claims before a deployment is live.
